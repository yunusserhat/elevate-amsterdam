Take part·pilot of the data-collection layer pilot

Your view of Amsterdam's streets.

Three short tasks: react to AI-edited street pictures (WP6), compare streets (WP3) and pick what should be measured first (WP1). A few minutes each, in any order.

Before you start
  • This is a pilot that shows how a project like ELEVATE could gather residents' views. It is not a research study, and answers will not be used as findings without an ethics-approved study.
  • It is anonymous: no name, e-mail or free text, and no IP address in the survey database. Your browser makes a random session number.
  • Answers go through this site's server into a PostgreSQL database (Supabase, EU). Nobody can read single answers through the site; results are published as totals only.
  • Your internet address is kept in memory for one minute to slow down floods of answers, and is never stored.
  • You can stop at any time; nothing is saved until you answer.
