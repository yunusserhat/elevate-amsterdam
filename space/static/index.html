Reading the labels

What is real here, and what is not.

real data The City's 2025 panoramas, CBS statistics, OpenStreetMap and the City's public-space reports. Model outputs are observations of images, not measurements of health, safety or walkability.

synthetic Generated here to the protocol's parameters, to test the analysis chain against a known answer. No person was tracked.

hypothetical Written by role-play, to be replaced by what workshops say.

idea Something I would bring to the project; not part of the protocol.
