What WP4 would add

Walk-alongs where the two sources disagree.

The protocol interviews about twenty participants on site and on walk-alongs, then triangulates the transcripts with the numbers. Buurten where reports and images disagree are the natural place to start: a resident can say what the camera missed, or why nobody reports what it saw.
